Local authority grant work

Bathroom adaptations through council grants.

If you or a family member needs a safer, more accessible bathroom, we carry out bathroom adaptation works that may qualify for support through Local Authority housing grant schemes.

From walk-in showers and wet rooms to practical, accessible bathroom layouts, we supply and fit solutions tailored to individual needs.

If you’re applying for a grant through Tipperary County Council or another local authority, get in touch to discuss the work required and arrange a quotation.
