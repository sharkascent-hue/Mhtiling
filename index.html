What you’re paying for

One en-suite, four details. Scroll to see them.

The bits that separate a bathroom that looks good on day one from one that still looks good in ten years.

01

  1. 01

    Backlit mirror, wired in first

    The cable runs inside the wall before a tile goes on, so there is no trailing flex and the switch is a touch sensor on the glass.

  2. 02

    Lit niche with mitred edges

    Cut into the wall, tanked, tiled with 45-degree mitred edges instead of plastic trim, and lit by a hidden LED strip.

  3. 03

    Linear drain with a true fall

    The floor is screeded to fall one way, tanked, and flood-tested before tiling. That is what keeps a wet room dry underneath.

  4. 04

    Wall-hung vanity, floor runs under

    Fixed to a hidden frame so the floor tile carries on underneath. Easier to clean, and a small room looks bigger.

How it works

Four steps from first call to finished bathroom.

Site visit & quote

We call out, measure up, talk through what you want and send a clear written quotation. No charge, no pressure.

Design & selection

We help you choose tiles, brassware, furniture and lighting that work together, and sort the ordering so everything lands on time.

Strip-out & build

Old suite out, plumbing and electrics first-fixed, walls and floors prepared, then tiling and fitting. Site tidy every evening.

Finish & handover

Sealing, grouting, silicone, final clean and a walk-through with you. We don’t leave until you’re happy with every detail.
