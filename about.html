MH Tiling & Bathroom Renovations is a local trade business that does one thing and does it properly.

Our story

Fewer bathrooms, each one done right.

MH started the way most good trade businesses do: one tiler, a van, and a reputation that spread by word of mouth around Tipperary. As the jobs grew from tiling into full bathrooms, we kept the same rule we started with. Do the whole room ourselves, do it properly, and leave it clean.

Because we only do bathrooms, we have solved the awkward corners, the out-of-square walls and the tricky drainage runs many times before. That experience shows in the finish, and in how smoothly the job runs while we are in your home.

The process

What happens, and when.

A bathroom renovation has a lot of moving parts. This is how we keep them in order so that you always know what is happening next.

We’d rather do fewer bathrooms and do each one properly than rush three at once.

MH Tiling & Bathroom Renovations
Week 0

Site visit and quotation

We measure up, talk through what you want and what the room allows, then send a written quote itemising what is included.

Weeks 1 to 3

Design and ordering

Tiles, sanitaryware, furniture and brassware chosen and ordered. Lead times are checked so nothing holds up the start date.

Day 1 to 2

Strip-out and first-fix

Old bathroom removed and skipped. Pipework and cabling moved to suit the new layout. Walls boarded and floor levelled.

Day 3 to 9

Tanking and tiling

Wet areas tanked, then walls and floors tiled. Niches, mitres and trims are built in as we go, not bolted on afterwards.

Day 10 to 13

Fitting and finishing

Shower, bath, WC, vanity, mirror and rail fitted. Grouting, silicone, electrics commissioned and tested.

Final day

Clean and handover

Deep clean, a walk-through with you, and a note of how to look after the finishes. We come back if anything needs attention.
