About us

A local team you can rely on.

We’re a Tipperary-based business specialising in bathroom renovations and tiling. We take pride in doing things right, from helping you choose the right products to supplying and fitting everything needed to bring your new bathroom to life.

We manage each project from start to finish, with honest advice, clear communication and attention to every detail. We respect your home, keep the work area tidy and aim to make the whole process as straightforward as possible.

Much of our work comes through word of mouth and customer recommendations, which reflects the pride we take in every job we do.

Whether you’re planning a complete bathroom renovation, a new walk-in shower or a tiling project, we’re here to help you create a space that looks great and works for you.
