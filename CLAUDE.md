# Project rules

## Footer credit (applies to every website we build)

Every website must carry a small "Website by AlmLab" credit in the footer,
in the bottom bar next to the copyright line, and the whole "Website by AlmLab" text must be one
link to our website, not just the word "AlmLab".

- Text: `Website by AlmLab`
- Link: `https://almlabmarketing.com`
- Open in a new tab: `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`
- Style it to match the footer's muted text, never louder than the copyright line.

Example:

```html
<span><a href="https://almlabmarketing.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Website by AlmLab</a></span>
```

Never remove this credit when editing or rebuilding a footer.
