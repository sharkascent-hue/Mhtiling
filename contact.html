Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Quick answers to the things people usually ask on the first call.

What do you need from me for a quote?
A few photos of the room as it is, rough measurements, and an idea of what you would like to change. From that we can give you a range by phone. For a firm written quote we visit, measure properly and talk it through.
How far ahead are you booked?
It varies through the year. Get in touch early if you have a date in mind, and we will tell you honestly when we can start.
Can I stay in the house during the work?
Yes, almost everyone does. If it is the only bathroom we plan the job so the toilet is out of action for as short a time as possible and we tell you exactly which days.
Do you need a deposit?
A deposit secures your start date and covers materials ordered for your job. The balance is paid in stages and on completion, all set out in the quotation.
