Common questions

Quick answers to what people ask most.

Do you remove the old bathroom?
Yes. Strip-out and disposal are included in every renovation quote.
Do I need a separate plumber or electrician?
No. We handle it all and price it as one job.
Can you supply the tiles and fittings?
Yes. We can supply everything, or fit what you have bought yourself.
How long does a bathroom take?
Most full bathrooms are finished in a week. En-suites and small rooms are quicker.
Do you do tiling only?
Yes. Floors, walls, splashbacks and feature walls.
Is the work guaranteed?
Yes. We stand over our work and come back if anything needs attention.
